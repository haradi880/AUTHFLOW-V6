Privacy Policy

AuthFlow processes account, profile, content, hiring, collaboration, notification, messaging, and security data to operate a professional developer and hiring platform.

Data We Collect

We store account identifiers, email, password hashes, verification tokens, profile fields, resumes or portfolio links, uploaded media, published content, job posts, job applications, recruiter responses, saved jobs, teams, invitations, collaboration requests, messages, notifications, moderation reports, blocks, audit events, and basic device/request metadata.

How We Use Data

Data is used for authentication, fraud prevention, profile discovery, hiring workflows, collaboration workflows, messaging, notifications, email delivery, moderation, analytics, platform reliability, and legal compliance.

Notification And Email Controls

In-app notifications are retained as account history with seen and read states. Email may be sent for verification, password reset, messages, comments, follows, likes, job applications, recruiter updates, team invitations, collaboration requests, and service announcements.

User Rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, export, restriction, or objection where applicable. Deletion requests may retain limited records needed for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal obligations.

Security

AuthFlow uses password hashing, session cookies with secure attributes in production, CSRF protection, rate limiting, SQLAlchemy parameterized queries, upload validation, security headers, login lockout controls, and audit logging. Production deployments should use managed secrets, HTTPS, encrypted backups, Redis-backed rate limits, monitored email queues, and centralized logging.

Cookies

Essential cookies support login, CSRF, session continuity, and remember-me authentication. Optional cookies should only run after consent. See the Cookie Policy.

Contact

For privacy, export, deletion, or security requests, contact the platform operator through support.
