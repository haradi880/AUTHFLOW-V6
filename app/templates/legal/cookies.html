Cookie Policy

HaradiBots uses cookies and local browser storage to keep the platform secure, authenticated, and usable.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies support sessions, CSRF protection, remember-me login, flash messages, and security controls. These are required for the app to work and cannot be disabled inside the product.

Optional Cookies

Optional analytics or marketing cookies should only load after the user accepts optional cookies. The current consent choice is stored in local browser storage as `haradibots_cookie_consent`.

Managing Cookies

Users can clear cookies and local storage from their browser settings. Clearing essential cookies may log the user out or require forms to be submitted again.

Production Guidance

Production deployments should document each analytics, support, payment, or advertising vendor before enabling optional cookies, including purpose, retention, processor role, and opt-out mechanism.
